- Print receipts created from the receipts feature
- Convert receipt data into ESC/POS commands
- Support:
  - 58mm paper
  - 80mm paper
- Print:

  - items
  - subtotal
  - tax
  - total
  - footer message
  - QR code placeholder
- Integrate with saved Bluetooth printer
- Update receipt print status after successful print
- Support receipt reprinting

- esc_pos_utils_plus
- print_bluetooth_thermal
- flutter_riverpod
